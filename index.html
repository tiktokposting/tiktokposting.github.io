Carousel Poster

This is a personal tool that schedules and publishes photo posts to the operator's own TikTok accounts using TikTok's official Content Posting API. It posts only content the operator uploads, to accounts the operator owns and has authorized.

It does not collect data from other TikTok users, does not browse or automate the TikTok app or website, and does not sell or share data.
