Privacy Policy

Last updated: REPLACE_DATE. This Privacy Policy explains how Carousel Poster ("the app", "we") handles information. The app is operated by YOUR_NAME for the purpose of publishing the operator's own content to the operator's own TikTok accounts.

Information we access

When you authorize the app, TikTok provides an access token and refresh token tied to your account, and basic profile information (such as your open ID and display name) under the user.info.basic scope. The app uses the video.publish scope to post photo content you provide to your account.

How we use it

Tokens are used solely to publish the content you schedule, on your instruction. Basic profile information is used only to confirm which account a post is going to. We do not use this information for advertising, profiling, or any purpose other than publishing your content.

Storage and retention

Access and refresh tokens are stored privately on a server controlled by the operator, solely to keep the connection active. You can revoke the app's access at any time in your TikTok settings, which invalidates the stored tokens.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties. The only data transmitted is the content and captions you schedule, sent to TikTok's official API to publish on your behalf.
